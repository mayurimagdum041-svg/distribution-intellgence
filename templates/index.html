Mathematical Formulation & Operational Assumptions

Complete documentation of objective functions, constraints, and methodologies

1. Problem Formulation (Mixed-Integer Linear Program)

The distribution optimization problem is formulated as a multi-echelon cost-minimization Mixed-Integer Linear Program (MILP):

$$\min \sum_{w \in W, s \in S, b \in B} \Big( c_{trans}(w,s,p) + \text{Penalty}_{FEFO}(b,s) \Big) \cdot X_{w,s,b} + \sum_{s \in S, p \in P} \text{Penalty}_{stockout}(p) \cdot U_{s,p}$$

2. Decision Variables

  • \(X_{w, s, b}\) (Integer \(\ge 0\)): Units of inventory batch \(b\) (of product \(p\)) transported from warehouse \(w\) to store \(s\).
  • \(U_{s, p}\) (Continuous \(\ge 0\)): Unmet customer demand (slack variable) for product \(p\) at store \(s\).

3. Operational Constraints

  • Warehouse Batch Supply Constraint: $$\sum_{s \in S} X_{w, s, b} \le \text{BatchQuantity}(b) \quad \forall (w, b)$$
  • Store Replenishment Demand Balance: $$\sum_{w \in W, b \in B_{p}} X_{w, s, b} + U_{s, p} = \text{NetDemandNeeded}(s, p) \quad \forall (s, p)$$
  • Transit Feasibility Constraint: If \(\text{TransitTime}(w, s) \ge \text{ExpiryDays}(b) - 0.5\), the variable is excluded (preventing transit spoilage).

4. First-Expired-First-Out (FEFO) Perishability Prioritization

For inventory batches with shelf life \(\le 4\) days, routing them to high-velocity stores that sell them immediately incurs a negative penalty (priority bonus), while routing them to slow stores incurs an economic spoilage penalty.

5. Multi-Echelon Safety Stock Formula

$$SS = Z \times \sqrt{L \cdot \sigma_D^2 + \bar{D}^2 \cdot \sigma_L^2}, \quad ROP = (\bar{D} \times L) + SS$$

Where \(Z = 1.645\) (95% service level), \(L\) is lead time, \(\bar{D}\) is daily demand rate, \(\sigma_D\) is demand standard deviation, and \(\sigma_L\) is lead time uncertainty.

6. Demand Forecasting Model

Autoregressive Ridge Regression with feature engineering including 1-day, 7-day, 14-day lags, 7-day rolling means and standard deviations, day-of-week seasonality, and promotional calendar lifts with 95% Confidence Intervals.

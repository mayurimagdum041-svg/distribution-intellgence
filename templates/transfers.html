Transfer Safeguards & Rules

Automated validation guarantees inventory integrity

1. Origin & Target Differentiation

Transfers where the source and destination facilities are identical are automatically rejected.

2. Overdraft Prevention

The system enforces source availability. Transfers that exceed on-hand stock at the origin location are blocked immediately.

3. ACID Database Atomicity

Source deduction and destination addition happen together in an atomic database transaction. If any error occurs, the entire transaction rolls back cleanly.
